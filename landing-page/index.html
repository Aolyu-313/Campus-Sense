Problem

Students and campus users often do not know whether a nearby outdoor or semi-outdoor space is comfortable for studying, resting, or staying. CampusSense turns environmental data and user reports into a quick, readable comfort score.

Key Features

Comfort Dashboard

Shows a real-time comfort score, location, weather, air quality, and movement state.

User Reports

Allows users to submit subjective feedback about their current environment.

History & Nearby

Users can review past reports and explore nearby environmental feedback.

How It Works

  1. The Flutter app reads GPS location and movement state from the device.
  2. The Spring Boot backend receives the location and requests weather and air quality data.
  3. The backend calculates a comfort score and stores reports in MySQL.
  4. The app displays the dashboard, history, and nearby comfort reports.
